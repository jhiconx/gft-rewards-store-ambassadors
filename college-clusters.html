    03 / Campus-to-store operations

    One accountable ambassador. Clear weekly actions.

    Proposed operating rhythm, not existing store commitments or guaranteed workload. GFT approves which locations can be approached.

    01

    Recruit on campus

    Start with campus career services, student clubs and bilingual community organizations. Confirm English/Spanish fluency, transportation and availability.

    02

    Train before visits

    Complete GFT Rewards platform training, sales messaging, checkout-phone-number flow, approved outreach rules and retail POS readiness checklist.

    03

    Visit in a cluster

    Schedule appointments, bundle nearby stops into the same field session and avoid assigning outlying stores without a second visit block.

    04

    Report and escalate

    Log manager contact, meeting outcome, POS platform, implementation requirements, follow-up date and the store's approved attribution.

    Existing compensation model

    Qualified ambassadors use the current written GFT program: $25 per approved store signup, $1 per verified unique first-promotion redemption, 2¢ per eligible ongoing redemption and $500 per completed approved territory roster. Actual eligibility and payment timing follow the signed agreement.
