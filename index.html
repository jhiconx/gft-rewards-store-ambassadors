Store-based ambassador compensation

Onboard stores. Support their first offer. Earn as they succeed.

Earn for qualifying, verified store introductions, eligible ongoing reward redemptions, and completion of your approved territory roster.

🤝
$25

One-time payment per qualifying store introduction

Earn $25 for each qualifying, verified introduction to a grocery store assigned to you, one time per store. The written program terms define qualifying introductions, verification and attribution. Store signup is a separate milestone.

🔁
1¢

Ongoing share per reward redemption

Earn $0.01 for each verified, eligible reward redemption at a store credited to you while that store remains part of the active program under your written agreement. This ongoing payment continues after the first promotion.

🏁
$500

One-time territory completion bonus

Earn a one-time $500 bonus when every store on your pre-approved territory roster completes qualifying signup and executes three qualifying promotional events. The roster, event criteria and completion requirements are agreed upon in writing before work begins.

The essentials

Quick questions

Do I need to speak both English and Spanish?

Yes. Ambassadors are expected to hold store-owner conversations and guide store teams through GFT Rewards onboarding in English and Spanish. GFT platform training is required before field assignments.

When do I earn the $25 introduction payment?

The one-time $25 incentive is earned for a qualifying, verified introduction to a grocery store assigned to you. A store signup is not required for this introduction incentive. The written program agreement specifies verification, eligibility and attribution requirements.

How does the ongoing 1¢ redemption payment work?

You earn 1 cent for each verified, eligible reward redemption at your attributed stores while they remain in the active program under your written agreement, including after their first promotion. The written plan defines eligibility, reversals, reporting, and payment timing.

How do I earn the $500 territory bonus?

The one-time $500 bonus is earned when every store on your pre-approved territory roster completes qualifying signup and executes three qualifying promotional events. All three events are required for each roster store. The approved roster, event criteria and completion rules are confirmed in writing before work begins.

Is a 20 × 20-mile territory exclusively mine?

Not automatically. These are proposed geographic planning zones. Final territory assignment, store attribution and any exclusivity are decided in the written ambassador agreement. Some proposed zones overlap.

Can I sign up any store right away?

No. Stores must be approved by the GFT team, including any necessary point-of-sale or technical readiness checks, before a qualifying signup or promotion can be completed.

Can college students participate in a verified internship?

Eligible students can request an internship track while completing the same ambassador duties. Work may include major-brand campaigns, retailer management, shopper loyalty, and account-specific loyalty. Hours and responsibilities may be documented and reviewed by a supervisor; the school determines whether participation meets its internship or credit requirements.

Is this a Sampling For Good role?

No. This is a separate grocery-store recruitment and onboarding program for GFT Rewards. Iconx.io remains the recruiting and field agency.
