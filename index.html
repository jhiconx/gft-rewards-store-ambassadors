Store-based ambassador compensation

Onboard stores. Support their first offer. Earn as they succeed.

The same three payout amounts as the campus program, adapted to verified grocery-store onboarding and first-promotion activity.

🤝
$25

One-time payment per signed-up store

Introduce a qualified store and arrange a meeting. When the store is approved by GFT Rewards and completes its qualifying platform signup, you earn one $25 payment. A meeting alone does not trigger the payout.

🏪
$1

Per verified unique redemption — first promotion

For each store attributed to you, earn $1 per verified, nonduplicate unique shopper redemption during that store's first eligible promotion only. The measurement window is defined in the written compensation plan.

🏁
$500

One-time territory completion bonus

Earn a final $500 bonus when every store on your pre-approved territory roster completes qualifying signup and its first eligible promotion. The store roster is agreed upon in writing before work begins.

The written compensation plan will define qualifying stores, verified nonduplicate redemptions, the first-promotion measurement window, attribution, territory rosters, payment timing, and any compensation legally required for work performed. Incentive payments do not replace wages owed under applicable law.

The essentials

Quick questions

Do I need to speak both English and Spanish?

Yes. Ambassadors are expected to hold store-owner conversations and guide store teams through GFT Rewards onboarding in English and Spanish. GFT platform training is required before field assignments.

Does setting up a meeting earn the $25 payment?

No. The $25 is earned once an approved, qualifying store attributed to you completes its GFT Rewards platform signup. Introductions and meetings are part of the work, but do not independently trigger the incentive.

What counts toward the $1 redemption payout?

Only verified, nonduplicate unique shopper redemptions from each attributed store's first eligible promotion, within the agreed measurement window. GFT Rewards records determine qualifying redemption counts under the written plan.

How do I earn the $500 territory bonus?

The one-time bonus is triggered after every store on your pre-approved territory roster completes qualifying registration and its first eligible promotion. The roster and completion rules must be confirmed in writing before work begins.

Is a 20 × 20-mile territory exclusively mine?

Not automatically. These are proposed geographic planning zones. Final territory assignment, store attribution and any exclusivity are decided in the written ambassador agreement. Some proposed zones overlap.

Can I sign up any store right away?

No. Stores must be approved by the GFT team, including any necessary point-of-sale or technical readiness checks, before a qualifying signup or promotion can be completed.

Is this a Sampling For Good role?

No. This is a separate grocery-store recruitment and onboarding program for GFT Rewards. Iconx.io remains the recruiting and field agency.
